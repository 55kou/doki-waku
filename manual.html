HOW TO PLAY

遊び方は、3ステップ

順番にサイコロを振って、止まったマスの効果を受けるだけ。あとは、どのルートを選ぶか、どこで勝負するかです。

01

職業を選ぶ

最初に人数（2〜6人）とNPCを選び、各プレイヤーが職業を選びます。職業ごとに給料やギャンブルの勝ち方が違います。

02

サイコロで進む

自分の番にサイコロを振り、出た目の数だけ進みます。分かれ道では、安定ルートか一攫千金ルートを選びます。

03

マスの効果を受ける

お金が増えるマス、減るマス、ギャンブル、風俗、イベントなど。ゴールしたときの所持金で順位が決まります。

基本ルール内容
スタート資金全員 10,000円。所持金に上限はありません。
給料日盤面に全部で7回。会社員と起業家は通過するだけでもらえます。ギャンブラーだけは、ぴったり止まったときのみです。
マイナス資金資金がマイナスでもゲームは続きます。ギャンブルは「前借り」になります（ギャンブルの章を参照）。
ゴールゴールマスに着くとその人は上がり。全員がゴールしたら結果発表です。

HOW TO PLAY

Three simple steps

Take turns rolling the dice and resolve the square you land on. The rest is deciding which route to take and where to make your stand.

01

Pick a job

At the start, choose the number of players (2–6) and which are NPCs, then each player picks a job. Pay and gambling strengths differ by job.

02

Roll and move

On your turn, roll the die and move that many squares. At forks, choose the Safe Route or the Jackpot Route.

03

Resolve the square

Squares can add money, take money, offer gambling, nightlife, events and more. Your ranking is decided by how much money you have at the goal.

Basic rulesDetails
Starting money¥10,000 for everyone. There is no cap on your money.
PaydayThere are 7 on the board. Office Workers and Entrepreneurs get paid just by passing through. Gamblers get paid only when they stop exactly on it.
Negative moneyThe game goes on even if you are in the red. Gambling becomes an "advance" (see the Gambling chapter).
GoalOnce you reach the goal square you are done. When everyone has finished, the results are shown.

GAMBLING

Six games. Time to gamble

When you land on a gambling square you can pay the stake and play, or pass. Stakes rise in later sections (¥2,000–8,000). Hi-Lo is fixed at ¥500.

GameRules
BlackjackClosest to 21 wins. A win pays +1× and blackjack pays +1.5×. There is a 35% chance the game is Free Bet Blackjack, where doubling and splitting are free.
Hi-LoGuess whether the next card is higher or lower. You can double up to 5 times.
BaccaratBet on Player, Banker or Tie. Player pays +1×, Banker +0.95×, Tie +8×.
RouletteRed/black pays +1×, dozens (3 choices) +2×, and 0 means the house takes everything. There is a 30% chance of "I want to win big right now…!!", which forces a single-number bet (place 5 chips on any numbers; a hit pays 36× per chip).
ChinchiroThe dealer and then you roll three dice, up to 3 times, and compete on the hand. See the hand table below.
Horse racingBet on Win (1st place) or Place (top 3, low payout). A hit pays the odds multiple. There is a 35% chance of a 10-horse G1 race, where you can also buy a Trifecta (1st–3rd in exact order).

You can gamble even when your money is negative. The play fee is borrowed and settled at the goal at 1.2× including interest.

Perks for streak winners

Win all 6 kinds of gambling and you become the Casino King; everyone else pays you ¥5,000 in tribute.

On a nightlife square each of the 5 venues is equally likely (20%). The Menesu secret option fee is free 25% / ¥1,000–5,000 (10% each) / ¥6,000–10,000 (5% each).

STDs

Once infected, you pay a treatment fee every turn (¥500 × the number of turns since infection). Landing exactly on a hospital square cures you for free. Chlamydia, syphilis and HIV can also heal on their own after a number of turns.

Marriage & children

Visit the same venue twice and you are forced to marry your partner there. If you catch an STD while married, you get divorced at the end of the turn.

  • When you marry you get candidiasis (−¥500 every turn). For uri-sen, a colostomy (−¥1,500 every turn)
  • Visit the same venue 3 times and a child is born. A child with your married partner costs ¥500 child-rearing money every turn; if you aren't married, it costs ¥800 child support with no custody
  • A divorce costs ¥10,000 in alimony and you may lose custody. Children are shown as emoji: child-support-paying 👦, non-paying 🐒, custody kept 👶

GOAL

Goal & winning

When everyone has reached the goal, advances are settled and the final standings are decided by final money, title bonuses included, from highest to lowest. The results screen also shows everyone's money over time as a line chart.

Comeback Golden Dice

In the second half of the board, the player in last place has a 20% chance of receiving a Golden Dice. You can pick any number.

Immune after the goal　Players who have already reached the goal are unaffected by anything other players do (tribute, duels, government handouts, infection attacks, and so on).
